# Check GDPR EU Agent — by openways.ai

> [!WARNING]
> **Not legal advice. A human is always responsible for the data.**
> This tool helps you find possible problems, but it can be wrong or out of date, and a clean result does not mean you comply. The data controller (you, your company, or your client) is solely responsible for compliance. The authors accept no liability. Read the full [DISCLAIMER](DISCLAIMER.md) before use.

A free, open-source agent for Claude Code and other AI assistants. It checks websites and codebases for **GDPR and ePrivacy (cookie) compliance** across EU countries:
- trackers that fire before consent,
- a missing "reject all" option on the first layer,
- dark patterns in the cookie banner,
- consent proof and withdrawal,
- third-party transfers,
- country-specific rules.

**Status: work in progress.** The research phase is running now and covers EU law and regulator practice from 2022 to 2026, country by country. Follow the [CHANGELOG](CHANGELOG.md) for releases.
